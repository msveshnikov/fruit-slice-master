# Fruit Slice Master 🗡️🍎 (built by [AutoCode](https://autocode.work) in 20 minutes)

Become a true Fruit Slice Master in this exciting arcade game!

## 🎮 FEATURES

-   Smooth sword-slicing mechanics with realistic cutting effects
-   Beautiful glowing blade trails follow your finger movements
-   8 different juicy fruits to slice: 🍎 🍊 🍉 🍍 🍌 🍐 🥝 🍇
-   Satisfying sword slashing sound effects
-   Arcade-style scoring system with combo multipliers
-   Stunning particle effects and animations
-   Monthly seasonal fruit themes
-   Multiple blade types (Standard, Golden, Diamond)
-   Zen mode for relaxed gameplay
-   Global Leaderboards System
-   No ads, completely free to play!

## 🎯 HOW TO PLAY

✓ Swipe your finger across the screen to slice fruits ✓ Create combos by cutting multiple fruits at
once ✓ Watch out for special bonus fruits ✓ Avoid missing fruits (-50 points penalty) ✓ Chain combos
for score multipliers ✓ Try to beat your high score! ✓ Unlock special blades through achievements
