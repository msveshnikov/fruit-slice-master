# Fruit Slice Master 🍉🗡️

A juicy 3D fruit-slicing arcade game made with Godot 4 and Blender.

**Play in the browser:** https://maxims.top/fruit/ · **Android APK:** https://maxims.top/fruit/FruitSliceMaster.apk

- Swipe to slice glossy 3D fruit — every fruit splits into two halves with a painted cut face, juice and splats.
- **6 painted worlds × 20 levels**: Bamboo Garden, Sunset Lagoon, Lantern Market, Frost Peak, Volcano Forge, Sky Garden.
  Goals change every level (slice N, score race, one fruit type, combos, survive the bombs, perfect run) and every
  world ends with a **giant boss fruit**.
- Power-up fruit: golden apple (double points), frost berry (slow time), star fruit (frenzy), pomegranate (multi-hit).
- Endless modes: Classic (3 lives), Arcade (60 s), Zen (90 s, no bombs).
- 8 blade trails unlocked with stars. Russian and English.

Everything is generated by code: fruit models, halves, textures and icons in Blender (`blender/scripts/`), effects
and sounds by Python tools (`tools/`), levels by `scripts/game.gd`. See `AGENTS.md` for test/build/deploy and
`assets/CREDITS.md` for music and sounds (CC0).

The original 2024 HTML/Cordova version lives in `legacy_cordova/`.
