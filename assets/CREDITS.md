## Art
World backgrounds, the title backdrop and the app icon were painted for this game with ChatGPT image generation
on the owner's account (originals in `concept/`). All fruit, halves, the bomb and fruit icons are built
procedurally in Blender (`blender/scripts/build_fruits.py`); effect textures by `tools/make_textures.py`.
