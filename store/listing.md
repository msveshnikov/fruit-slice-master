Title (≤30): Fruit Slice Master

Short (≤80): Slice juicy 3D fruit in 120 levels, beat giant boss fruit, unlock 8 blades!

Full:
Swipe across glossy 3D fruit and slice it clean in two!

Every fruit really splits into halves with its own cut face: the star of an apple core, watermelon seeds, orange segments, kiwi rings. Juice sprays, splashes land on the scenery and the halves tumble toward you. Chain three or more fruit in one swipe for big combos, and never, ever touch a bomb.

🍉 120 LEVELS IN 6 PAINTED WORLDS
Bamboo Garden, Sunset Lagoon, Lantern Market, Frost Peak, Volcano Forge and Sky Garden. Every level has its own goal: slice a number of fruit, race for a score, cut only one kind of fruit, make combos, survive a rain of bombs or finish without dropping a single fruit.

👑 GIANT BOSS FRUIT
Each world ends with a huge boss fruit that drifts across the sky and spits bombs at you. Slash it again and again until it bursts!

⚡ POWER-UP FRUIT
• Golden apple: double points
• Frost berry: slows time down
• Star fruit: a frenzy of fruit from both sides
• Pomegranate: freezes in the air, every swipe is another hit

🎮 THREE ENDLESS MODES
• Classic: three lives, drop three fruit and it's over
• Arcade: 60 seconds of combos and power-ups
• Zen: 90 relaxed seconds, no bombs

🗡️ 8 BLADE TRAILS
Collect stars to unlock Fire, Ice, Jade, Neon, Gold, Rainbow and Void blades.

• 16 kinds of fruit, all modelled in 3D
• Juicy effects, slow motion on big combos, satisfying sounds
• Works offline, Russian and English
• No ads, free to play
